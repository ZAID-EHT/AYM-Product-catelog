# Project preferences

- Product navigation uses swipe left for the next product and swipe right for the previous product. Keep on-screen hints and drag transitions consistent with this direction.
- Shared and downloaded product PNGs must contain only the product photograph and full product name. Do not add prices, currency, price labels, SKUs, product numbers, AYM branding, headers, footers or other text. Keep the original photograph unchanged, including any text already embedded in it.
- This export preference does not remove prices or product codes from the website itself.
- Preserve the original Excel workbook and files in `images/`.
